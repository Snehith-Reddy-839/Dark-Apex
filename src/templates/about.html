ℹ️ About HEMOSCAN

HEMOSCAN is a lightweight anemia screening web app designed to make basic CBC-based risk assessment more accessible and understandable.

The platform combines a prediction workflow, dataset exploration, and an AI educational assistant in a single Flask application.

What HEMOSCAN Includes

  • •Patient assessment form for anemia risk estimation using core blood parameters
  • •Dataset explorer with charts, column details, and downloadable CSV
  • •AI assistant for educational guidance on anemia and interpretation basics
  • •Responsive interface with light/dark theme support

Clinical Notice

HEMOSCAN is intended for learning and screening support only.

It does not provide diagnosis, prescriptions, or emergency triage. Always consult a licensed clinician for medical decisions.
