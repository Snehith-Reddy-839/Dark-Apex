Understanding Anemia

Anemia is a condition where your blood lacks enough healthy red blood cells to carry adequate oxygen to your body's tissues.

Start Anemia Analysis

What Is Anemia?

Anemia occurs when your blood does not have enough hemoglobin, leading to fatigue, weakness and pale skin.

Causes

Iron deficiency, vitamin B12 deficiency, chronic diseases, and genetic conditions are common causes.

Symptoms

Shortness of breath, dizziness, cold hands and feet, headaches and irregular heartbeat.

Iron Deficiency Anemia

The most common type, caused by lack of iron in the body.

Vitamin Deficiency Anemia

Caused by lack of vitamin B12 or folate.

Diagnosis

Diagnosed using complete blood count (CBC) and hemoglobin tests.

Treatment

Iron supplements, dietary changes, vitamin injections or medication.
